Privacy request

Request deletion of your stika data

Use this page to prepare an email to GREASON TECH SOLUTIONS (PTY) LTD requesting deletion of information stika holds about you.

Current service status: TikTok export is disabled in the current app build. Stika does not currently receive or retain production TikTok exports. You can still use this channel to ask us to check for and delete any information associated with you.

1. Prepare your request

Enter a reply-to email and choose what you want us to do. The information below stays in this page until you choose to open your email app. Review the message there and send it to submit your request.

Next step

Send the email to submit your request

Your email app should open with a message addressed to support@studenthubmarketing.co.za. The request is not submitted until you send that message.

After support receives it, we will verify the request, check for information stika holds, delete any TikTok-sourced data we control, and reply to confirm what was done. If we hold no such data, we will say so in our reply.

Do not email your TikTok password, login code, or one-time security code. This page does not send or store your request.

Return to support information

2. How the request is handled

  1. Support receives your email and verifies that the request is yours. We may ask for limited information needed to find the relevant app session.
  2. We check the information Stika controls. The current app does not connect to TikTok or keep production exports.
  3. If we hold TikTok-sourced data associated with your request, we delete it from systems we control and reply to confirm the result. If we hold none, we confirm that instead.

For other privacy questions, contact support@studenthubmarketing.co.za. Read the Privacy Policy for more about Stika’s current data handling.
